1 ns ≈ 300 mm in air, ≈ 207 mm in PTFE (εr ≈ 2.1). A VNA electrical-delay entry is this time delay, opposite sign of the fixture.

Formulas

vf = 1 / √εr
λg = c / (f √εr)
τ = ℓ √εr / c
θ = 360 · ℓ / λg

Bench notes

  • Port extension / electrical delay removes fixture delay after a cal at the coax connector.
  • λ/4 at the DUT is a classic bias-tee / stub check.
  • FR4 εr is messy (≈ 4.2–4.6 and frequency-dependent). Treat it as a starting guess.
  • Phase wrapping on the VNA: 360° is one guided wavelength.
