dBm ↔ VOPP

Peak-to-peak voltage from source power, and the reverse. CW sine into a matched load. Single-ended or complementary differential drive.

Source power per port
dBm
VOPP (SE pk-pk) sine

Assumptions

  • Continuous-wave sinusoid, no DC offset.
  • Matched real load. Default Z0 = 50 Ω.
  • dBm is available power per VNA port — the source-power setting.
  • Differential: equal amplitude, 180° ports. VOPP is V+ − V−, peak-to-peak.
  • If you drive a pair through a balun from one port, subtract the balun / hybrid loss separately.

Formulas

PW = 10(dBm/10) / 1000
Vrms = √(PW · Z0)
VOPPSE = 2√2 · Vrms
VOPPdiff = 2 · VOPPSE
Zdiff = 2 · Z0  ·  Ptotal = 2 · Pport

Arrow up/down on the dBm field steps 1 dB. Hold Shift for 0.1 dB.
